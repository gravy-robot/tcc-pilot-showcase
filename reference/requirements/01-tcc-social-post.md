# Mockup 1 requirements: TCC social post (Stage 1 Notice, TCC-owned)

Status: agreed with Brandon, Nov 6, 2026. Build after the hub.

## What it shows
Dr. Maya scrolling LinkedIn on her phone between appointments and seeing a TCC post that reshares Dr. Courtney Howard's wildfire-smoke advice. It proves "doctors talk about this, practically, without politics" and leads to the starter pack.

## Layout (option B)
- Phone frame, 390×844, showing the post with carousel slide 1 visible.
- To the right, all three carousel slides laid out side by side at readable size.
- Numbered annotations in the margin (1, 2, 3…) tied to markers on the phone and slides. Each says which journey insight or principle drives that detail.

## Banner
Top banner, same pattern as every page: "Mockup · TCC social post · Stage 1 Notice · TCC-owned · ← Back to hub" (links to `../index.html`).

## Feed chrome
- Generic LinkedIn-style chrome. No LinkedIn logo or wordmark. A small label: "Illustration of a LinkedIn-style feed."
- Social-proof line above the post with fictional names, e.g. "Dr. Priya N. and 2 other clinicians you know reacted."
- Poster: Talking Climate Collaborative, using the TCC logo/mark from the design system.

## Post copy
- Paraphrase Howard's advice. At most one direct quote, under 15 words, credited to Dr. Courtney Howard and linked to the CP24 article (URL in `audit.md`).
- Follows the 8 Principles: personal, plain words, one doable step, no doom, no partisan cues.
- Short caption naming why it works, e.g. a doctor, local and practical, three steps anyone can take today.

## Carousel (TCC design system)
1. **Quote card.** "One of the biggest public health threats that we face." Name and role: Dr. Courtney Howard, emergency physician. No photo.
2. **Her three steps.** Close windows. Filter the air. N95 masks for higher-risk people.
3. **Why this works.** Tags: Personal, Doable, Trustworthy (principle names from `principles/`). CTA button: "Get 3 patient-ready examples" (leads to the starter-pack sign-up; static, not clickable beyond an `href="#"` or hub link).

## Rules
- No real photos of people anywhere. Dr. Maya's avatar is an illustrated "M".
- Static, non-interactive.
- Annotations should cite: Stage 1 pain point (content reads as political; little time), the "proof it's normal" job, LinkedIn as top platform (research-facts.md), and the "Get 3 patient-ready examples" conversion point from Stage 3.
