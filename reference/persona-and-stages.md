# Persona, stages and pilot loop: health professionals

Source of truth: `maps/Main.dc.html` (journey map) and `maps/Touchpoints.dc.html` (touchpoint map). This file restates them in plain text so you don't have to parse the HTML.

## Persona: Dr. Maya, family physician (composite)

- Busy community practice.
- Sees heat and smoke in her older and asthmatic patients.
- Posts on LinkedIn a few times a month.
- Drafts in an AI chatbot on her phone.
- Makes clinic handouts in Canva.
- Belongs to a medical society.
- Wary of anything that looks partisan.

She is illustrative, not a real person. Never give her a photo. Use an illustrated initial ("M") as her avatar.

## The seven stages (shared by every segment)

| # | Stage | Phase | Her goal | Her voice (illustrative) | Pain point | Curated content's job |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Notice | Marketing funnel | Understand why patients do worse in heat and smoke, and whether doctors should speak | "This is showing up in my exam room." | Climate content reads as political; little time | Proof it's normal: Howard on wildfire smoke |
| 2 | Explore | Marketing funnel | Find credible, practical guidance for clinicians, not activists | "Is this for clinicians, or for activists?" | Nothing filters by profession, topic or place | Hero example: Lawson's heat-dome speech leads a filtered health library |
| 3 | Join | Marketing funnel | Get ongoing help without a big time commitment | "I can give this five minutes a week." | Sign-ups ask a lot and promise vague benefits | The offer: "Get 3 patient-ready examples" starter pack |
| 4 | Learn | Training | Learn what works in minutes | "Show me good examples. Don't lecture me." | Training ranked low (27%) vs. "what works" evidence (54%) | Contrast pairs: Lawson vs. Seattle op-ed; Howard vs. Vossler |
| 5 | Create | Enablement | Draft a post, op-ed or handout quickly, in her own voice | "I don't want to sound like a doomer, or partisan." | AI drafts come back generic, jargon-heavy or doom-laden | In-tool references: examples load into her chatbot as models |
| 6 | Share | Enablement | Publish without risk to her job or patients' trust | "Will my hospital sign off on this?" | Employer gatekeepers; good health videos got 20 and 55 views | Reassurance and reach; TCC reposts strong pieces |
| 7 | Return and connect | Network | Know it made a difference; find peers | "Other doctors like me are doing this, too." | Most communicators work alone | Contribution loop: her piece joins the library; monthly digest |

## The four pilot candidates (a loop)

| Order | Touchpoint | Stage | Lane | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | TCC social posts (LinkedIn) | 1 Notice | TCC-owned | Reshares Howard's smoke advice with a "why this works" caption; CTA to the starter pack |
| 2 | Health library on talkingclimate.com (mobile web) | 2 Explore | TCC-owned | Lawson's speech as hero; filters by role, topic and place |
| 3 | AI chatbot with curated examples | 5 Create | Messenger's own tools | Curated examples as models, so drafts avoid jargon, doom and partisanship |
| 4 | Dr. Maya's LinkedIn / YouTube post | 6 Share | Messenger's own tools | Goes live with a "made with TCC's health examples" link back to the library; peer clinicians see it and convert |

Why this loop: #4 has the highest K-factor of the Share touchpoints. A clinician's own post reaches peer clinicians (homophily), and the conversion path back to #2 is short. Employer comms review is runner-up (one approval reused across a health system) but is not a pilot.
