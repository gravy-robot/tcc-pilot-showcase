Learning case

“I voted” post

Jessie Diggins

Olympic cross-country skier
  • Voting
  • United States
  • 2026
  • Social post

What works, and what to fix

So-so
  • P1+Personal: strong
  • P2+Accessible: strong
  • P3+Empowering: strong
  • P4+Doable: strong
  • P5~Collective: mixed
  • P6–Normal: weak
  • P7+Trustworthy: strong
  • P8+For everyone: strong

A strong personal reason to vote and an easy step.

What to watch (learning case)

It leads with 11.2 million environmentalists not voting, which tells readers the behavior is rare (P6 weak). Fix: lead with her own reason, or with how many people do vote.
