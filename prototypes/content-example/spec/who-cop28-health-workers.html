  4. What works, and what to fix

    So-so
    • P1+Personal: strong
    • P2~Accessible: mixed
    • P3~Empowering: mixed
    • P4–Doable: weak
    • P5+Collective: strong
    • P6+Normal: strong
    • P7+Trustworthy: strong
    • P8~For everyone: mixed

    Health workers from several countries take turns naming what climate change is doing to their own patients. The chorus makes speaking up feel normal for clinicians, and the WHO name adds trust. It's a model for anyone who speaks for an organization.

    What to watch (learning case)

    It ends on alarm with no step for viewers (P4), and the crisis framing never turns to what's working (P3). Fix: keep the chorus and end with one thing viewers can do. About 57,000 plays and 219 likes on TikTok.

  5. Reusable pattern

    This is a learning case, so there’s no pattern to borrow as is. Its structure as published:

    1. Hook: everyone is at risk
    2. Chorus: each health worker names what they see in patients
    3. Systems: damaged clinics make care harder
    4. Close: people can't be healthy on a sick planet

    For-everyone note

    Names fossil fuels plainly but blames no party or person. The COP28 setting may read as political to some viewers.
