Doctors on smoke and heat: practical or partisan

Both start from health. One ends with three steps for today; the other turns into a fight about the President.

The second piece is a learning case, not a callout of its authors (rule R4). Use this page as a draft precedent sheet: print it or save it as PDF.

What to watch

One slip: jargon about particle size in microns. Try “tiny particles that reach deep into the lungs.”
