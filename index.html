TCC Curated Content: The page illustrates how example content can help create effective climate communicators

How curated examples help one trusted healthcare messenger, from first notice to sharing her own post.

Example user & use case

Healthcare: A doctor who sees the impact of climate in the exam room

Health professionals are trusted, and few climate communicators reach them yet. This scenario follows one of them.

Segment

Health professionals

An expansion opportunity for TCC. Doctors and nurses already talk to patients about heat and smoke. Few groups help them say it in public.

19%
of communicators target the health sector, in the TCC/AU research.

Persona

Dr. Maya, family physician

  • Busy community practice.
  • Sees heat and smoke in her older and asthmatic patients.
  • Posts on LinkedIn a few times a month.
  • Drafts in an AI chatbot on her phone. Makes clinic handouts in Canva.
  • Belongs to a medical society.
  • Wary of anything that looks partisan.

Dr. Maya is a composite, not a real person. She stands in for the doctors in the landscape research.
